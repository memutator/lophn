# lophn 0.19.0

Apps can now put their screens in more places around the workspace window, and the window gets a taskbar to hold them.

## New
- A taskbar along the bottom of the workspace window. On the left are global floats and the current desk's floats; on the right are tray items. Turn it off in Settings › Appearance.
- Apps can show a tray item in the workspace window. Clicking it opens a popup with the app's screen. The app sets the short text and tone shown on the item.
- Apps can offer a panel for the right sidebar. Turn it on in Settings, and it appears in the right sidebar of every workspace.
- A floating app panel can be made global. It stays open when you switch desks or workspaces.
- An agent can open an app screen with arguments for it.

## Changed
- The right sidebar remembers whether it is open separately for each workspace.
- The + menus in the sidebar and the tab bar show the same list of agents, with the same icons. The tab bar's + menu now also lists coding agents.
- Other workspaces' rows in the sidebar get a settings button and a + menu. Picking an agent or settings there switches to that workspace and opens it.
- The action buttons in the Workspaces header and on each workspace row line up and share one size.
- Moving a floating app screen between a desk and the global area reloads it once. Clicking a desk float collapses an unpinned global float.
- The tray popup reloads the app's screen each time it opens.

## Fixed
- Menus, popovers and dialogs no longer leave a focus ring on their trigger or first button after you open or close them with the mouse. Keyboard use still shows the ring.

## Action needed
- Apps you made yourself must switch to the new screen placement settings (the old "full" size becomes "tab") and be released again. An app released with the old size settings does not open in this version until it is released again.
